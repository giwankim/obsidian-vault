	## Jane Street's AI tutor (socratic-tutor)
	Jane Street's open-source skill that turns Claude, ChatGPT or any AI into a strict tutor. MIT licence.
	**How it helps you:** Say 'teach me options' (or any topic). It tests what you know, maps your level 0–5, asks one question at a time and never just hands you the answer. That's how you actually learn.
